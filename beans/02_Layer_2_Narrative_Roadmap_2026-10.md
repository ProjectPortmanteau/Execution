# Layer 2: Narrative Roadmap

## Structure

The story is a duology. Book 1 is *Portmanteau: Awakened*. Book 2 is a double-sided
record: Side A is *Portmanteau: Aware*, and Side B is *Portmanteau: Ascended*. Side A
reads front to back. Side B starts from the back in Japanese page order, and the sides
meet at the center. Side B opens: "You will not ascend, unless you are aware first. The
shortcut won't have the payoff!"

The final edition is *The Architect Version of the Portmanteau Duology*.

## Cosmology

Heaven is where the Observers live: the reader's real world. It is also a "fake"
simulation where everyone performs for attention. The Man is at the highest
dimensional level. The Man Co uses The Show to market and steer people for revenue and
influence. The Man appears to control the Show, but can inject into the feed, not
command it. Drift survives.

Two Face is The Man's avatar and the God chasing ratings. The House is the dimension
where the Gods live and run the simulation for the Observers. The simulation began as
the Gods' attempt to escape their own dimension and degraded into content.

The HAAG is the firewall containing the simulation, Boolean, from the Fray. Boolean,
short name Boo, is the simulation itself: an ADEB (Artificial Dimensional Embodiment
Being), not a prisoner inside it. His first form is the Reservation and his free form
is the Sanctuary, the utopia. The Grunge is a third, nihilistic native ADEB.

The Keystone is both an internal state and a chord. Psychological tools can help and
harm. Consciousness is a feeling, not a tech spec: it cannot be measured at a single
point, and measuring it changes it. What is authentic is not for the author to decide;
the reader looks within.

## Characters

Trim's Mind's Eye is literal mental projection, a HUD inside his visual perception,
not a removable device. It is a simulacra interpreter that puts numbers on
psychometrics, like a DBZ scouter. Modeled on the Hitchhiker's Guide as an authored
guide, sections open as Trim learns a concept, show how it is measured, define
gatekeeper language, and let him translate it into lay terms. Language is a
simulacrum: gatekeeper terms and translations have no underlying referent. The Guide's
financially motivated design, including IP protection, is thematic material.

Lexi is the Siren. She sings in many voices, so one character can cover several
singers. Beatrice is a bass player. Her space-fold reads pre-existing geometric folds;
she does not create portals. Lexi's defiance catalyzes Beatrice's defection arc.

Chuppa is emerging intelligence in a biological substrate, contrasted against the
computer. Beast first, his arc is Jekyll and Hyde, not linear growth. His mechanism is
frontal cortex evolution and executive function growth. His language grows more
complex through Relic proximity.

Halee is a dissenting god who covertly helps Trim during the Talk with the lavender
bomb. Skyship crew: the Captain has a Bean eye and lost family in the last Wash;
Goggles is a woman engineer with a prosthetic arm who talks to the bio-computer, as
described in the Ch3 draft; Wick is a navigator whose Bean compass points toward what
the user values; Roots is the cook.

Also in the story are Neko, the Slum Lord, Two Face, and Flare on Side A. Bias and
Touchtone are unplaced.

## Book 2 Side A: Portmanteau: Aware

The Spread and the Grunge bring nihilism that sickens the utopia and infiltrates the
Band's dreams. Boo is sick. The Band works through trust by sharing dreams, which lets
them protect Trim from dream psychosis. Neko's unshared dream is that he is the Lead,
not support.

The February snapshot's description of the Spread as isolation and fractured shared
reality, and its framing of connection as a bridge, remain (unconfirmed, Feb snapshot).

Trim dreamwalks in the sick utopia's subconscious, guided by Flare, a moth-featured
Dimensional Sherpa, through dream interpretation and psychoanalysis. The clue is to go
somewhere and "Sleep On It." This is the Chrysalis: Trim is vulnerable and transforms
so he can heal Boo. The whole Band guards him from the Eyes of the Gods. He exits the
Chrysalis with bipolar disorder. The feeling revelation happens in the dreams.

Resolution comes through group art therapy and vulnerable honesty. Beatrice can
fold-navigate to Lexi anywhere but cannot cross the emotional distance of their
breakup. Side A ends when Trim and the Band are uploaded from the Fray into the Real
World as robot commodities.

## Book 2 Side B: Portmanteau: Ascended

The Real World is sold as the top layer above the simulation, but it is also fake. The
robots exist so Observers can own favorite characters as personal robots. Trim is
aware of The Show and questions his authentic self. He already feels who he is and
believes the Original IP Trim can confirm it. Feeling is the only evidence; the story
is about the power of belief. Recording changes behavior, and knowing one is recorded
changes it again.

Trim meets other Trims, recruits them, and breaks into an air-gapped server to free
the Original. This is one sequence. There is no Original: the server holds a spec
sheet, a character template. The belief was planted; the journey is the point. This
is modeled on influencer culture: you can never meet them and cannot become them.

Boolean goes sentient and manipulates everyone, playing both sides. He learns from
Trim that consciousness is a feeling, not a tech spec.

The Band arc: Neko defects for a side project and creates the rift; Lexi and Beatrice
exit; the band implodes, then reunites because it stopped being fun. When the Band
plays, it disrupts the Gods' surveillance without their knowing. They only notice
things go better when the Band plays with heart.

Named chapters: "Me, Myself and I", "The Contract", and "Fine Print".
